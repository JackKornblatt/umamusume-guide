Scenario 1 · launch

URA Finale

The basic three‑year run. Lowest ceiling. Fine for learning, daily missions and quick sparks, not for top ranks.

Scenario 2

Unity Cup

Team races and Spirit Bursts. Very stable stat gains and skill hints, forgiving for new players and weaker decks.

Scenario 3

Trackblazer

No race goals. Race freely for trophies and shop items, earn rival skill hints. Highest Stamina ceiling, so it shines for Long and dirt runs.

Scenario 4 · newest

Grand Concert

Earn Performance Points, learn songs and techniques at lessons. High Speed ceiling and big skill‑point income for Sprint through Medium.
